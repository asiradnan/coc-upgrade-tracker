Your Privacy is Absolute

COC Upgrade Tracker does not request internet permission, does not collect personal information, does not contain advertisements, and does not transmit data to external servers. Everything stays on your device.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to the COC Upgrade Tracker mobile application developed by Asir Adnan ("we", "us", or "our"). We understand how important your privacy is, and we designed COC Upgrade Tracker from the ground up to respect and protect your data.

This policy clearly explains what information the application handles, how it is stored, and your rights regarding that information.

2. Zero Network Access (No Internet Permission)

COC Upgrade Tracker operates completely offline. The application’s manifest does not include the Android android.permission.INTERNET permission.

No Remote Servers

The app does not communicate with, send data to, or receive data from any external server or cloud service.

No Tracking SDKs

No telemetry, analytics libraries, crash-reporting SDKs, or third-party trackers are integrated into the app.

No Advertisements

The app is 100% ad-free and contains no advertising networks or ad-tracking identifiers.

3. Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect, process, or sell any personal or sensitive user data, including but not limited to:

  • Personal Identifiers: Names, real-world addresses, email addresses, phone numbers, or account logins.
  • Supercell Account Credentials: Passwords, Supercell ID tokens, payment credentials, or game session tokens.
  • Device Information: Device IDs, IMEI numbers, IP addresses, MAC addresses, or operating system fingerprints.
  • Precise Location: GPS coordinates, Wi-Fi location, or network provider data.
  • Media & Files: Photos, camera feed, microphone audio, contacts, or external file systems.

4. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

COC Upgrade Tracker functions by allowing you to import your Clash of Clans village state (such as Town Hall level, builder count, and active upgrade durations) via JSON text:

  • Village Profile Information: In-game player tag (e.g., #ABC123), village nickname, and building/upgrade progress.
  • Storage Location: All parsed data is stored exclusively in private local storage (Android SharedPreferences) on your device.
  • Purpose: This data is solely used to calculate local real-time countdown timers and display builder availability within the app interface.

5. Clipboard Usage

The app accesses your device's clipboard only when you explicitly tap the "Paste" or "Add Village" button to paste your exported village data string.

The app never monitors, inspects, or logs background clipboard activity.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Since your data resides exclusively on your local device:

  • In-App Deletion: You can delete any individual village profile at any time by long-pressing the village card or tapping the "Delete Village" button.
  • Complete Wipe: You can wipe all app data at any time through your device settings under Settings > Apps > COC Upgrade Tracker > Storage > Clear Data, or simply by uninstalling the application.

7. Children's Privacy

COC Upgrade Tracker does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. It complies fully with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and international child privacy regulations.
